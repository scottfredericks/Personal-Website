<section class="interests">

## My Interests

### Programming

Python is my go-to choice for most projects. I also work with Rust, JavaScript, C++, and other
languages. Beyond writing code, I have professional experience with testing, DevOps, feature planning, and
UX.

### Physics and Mathematics

I've always been drawn to foundational theory, especially geometric approaches to quantum gravity including [geometrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometrodynamics) and [noncommutative geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_geometry). I also have some experience with mathematical crystallography from my work on [PyXtal](https://github.com/MaterSim/PyXtal).

### Education

I enjoy teaching and mentoring others. Through my [blog](/blog) and other content, I aim to visualize complex ideas and make their core concepts accessible to everyone.

### Game Dev

I first experimented with game dev after receiving a copy of [QuickBASIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QuickBASIC) on a CD in junior high. Since then, I've built demos in various languages, and remain interested in creating larger projects using [Godot](https://godotengine.org/), [Bevy](https://bevy.org/), or [PICO-8](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php).

</section>
